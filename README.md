## ASK

**BUSINESS TASK:**  
Assess how financial performance, profitability trends, and valuation metrics of 20 publicly listed fashion companies developed from 2020–2025, and compare them using sales vs EBIT, sales growth vs EBIT%, and P/E vs P/B.

**THREE FIGURES NEEDED FOR THE ANALYSIS:**

**1. Sales × EBIT**
- Shows business performance:
- Sales = company size
- EBIT = operating profit
- Reveals who is big, who is profitable, and who is both.

**2. Sales Growth Change × EBIT % Change**
- Sales growth change = revenue acceleration or slowdown
- EBIT % change = margin expansion or contraction
- Reveals who is improving, stable, or weakening.

**3. P/E × P/B**
- P/E = price of earnings
- P/B = price relative to net assets
- Shows valuation. Reveals which companies are expensive, cheap, or mispriced.

**STAKEHOLDER:**
Corporate Finance Executive — evaluates financial health, profitability, and valuation relative to competitors.

## PREPARE

- Publicly listed companies were included to ensure accessible and consistent financial data.
- Financial values were collected from annual reports on each company’s website.
- Dataset follows the ROCCC approach (Reliable, Original, Comprehensive, Current, Cited) since the data was take from the publicly listed companies annual reports.  

## PROCESS

- Values were double‑checked to minimize human error.
- Currencies were verified to be consistent.
- Excel formulas were validated for correctness.

## ANALYZE

- Python and Tableau were used to clean, transform, and visualize the data.
- The three figures were generated to reveal performance, momentum, and valuation insights.
